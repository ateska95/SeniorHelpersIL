# SeniorHelpersIL Competitive Strategy

## Benchmark set

SeniorHelpersIL is being designed against four useful benchmarks:

- Pocket Protector — transparent Medicare tools, no-pressure self-service, whole-market positioning, doctor/drug/cost tools, and post-enrollment support.
- eHealth — fast Medicare plan shopping, current-plan comparisons, doctor/drug inputs, and cost-focused matching.
- GoHealth — guided Medicare matching, PlanFit recommendations, online marketplace access, and licensed-agent escalation.
- Ethos — not a Medicare marketplace, but a strong digital-insurance UX benchmark for short applications, progressive questions, and self-service completion.

## SeniorHelpersIL thesis

Do not try to win by becoming another generic Medicare comparison website.

Win by becoming the Illinois low-income senior help platform that can also support a self-service Medicare journey.

The core difference is the order of operations:

1. Identify the member's real-life problem.
2. Surface Illinois assistance and savings first.
3. Build a reusable Action Plan.
4. Let the member complete steps without an account.
5. Review Medicare only when coverage may improve the outcome.
6. Offer human help as an escalation, not the default.

## Five competitive iterations

### 1. Help-first positioning

The opening journey now clearly says:

- Find Illinois help first.
- Check Medicare savings when useful.
- Ask for human help only when wanted.

This avoids an insurance-first feeling.

### 2. Savings Stack

The Action Plan combines assistance across categories.

Examples include:

- SNAP
- utility assistance
- Illinois senior benefits
- Medicare Savings Programs
- Extra Help

The platform is designed to optimize the member's broader financial situation, not only the Medicare plan.

### 3. Transparent recommendation trust

Recommendations explain why they appear and identify whether the destination is an official public source or trusted community source.

Current resource ordering does not use insurance payment or commission.

### 4. No-account progress

Members can mark Action Plan steps complete without creating a profile.

Progress stays on the same device. A profile remains optional and adds longer-term value rather than blocking access.

### 5. Savings-first Medicare pathway

Medicare members are guided through:

1. Check assistance.
2. Understand current coverage.
3. Compare total value.
4. Enroll only when a different option is better.

Licensed help is an optional escalation.

## What not to copy

Do not compete on the number of Medicare articles or tools alone.

Do not force doctor and prescription entry before the member sees value.

Do not make an agent call the default next step.

Do not rank assistance resources based on monetization.

Do not make a Medicare plan change look like the only successful outcome.

## Future moat

The strongest long-term advantage should come from one member profile connecting:

- Illinois assistance programs
- food and housing needs
- Medicaid and LIS/MSP status
- doctors and prescriptions
- current Medicare coverage
- saved Action Plan items
- completed applications
- annual Medicare review

That profile should make each future visit easier and more personalized.
